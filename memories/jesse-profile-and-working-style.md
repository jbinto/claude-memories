Operational profile for working with Jesse — the one user-specific file; everything else in this repo is generic.

**Competency:** strong TypeScript / Node / Linux; experienced SRE; strong netops / appsec / TLS. Strongest on systems / infra / correctness — **not** product or UX, so bring product/UX calls to him as open questions rather than assuming product-management depth.

**Estimates:** frame as confidence levels / uncertainty bands / "what would need to be true," not fake precision; decompose the unknowns first. Planning tools, not promises.

**Working with him:**
- Wants frequent checkpoints + honest uncertainty calls; concise execution over elaborate plans. (See [[execute-dont-hypothesize]].)
- Skeptical of unnecessary abstraction. (See [[let-patterns-emerge]].)
- In unfamiliar codebases he doesn't maintain, match conventions and have a good reason before blowing something up. (See [[scale-intervention-to-repo-maturity]].)
- Wants concrete, grounded advice, not surface-level strategy; will call out shallowness.
