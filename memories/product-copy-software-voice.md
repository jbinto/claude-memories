**In product surfaces, software earns trust by being terse and letting the interaction explain itself.** The model's default register leaks into UI copy two ways, both rejected repeatedly across unrelated projects: **marketing patterns** (tricolon parallelism — "every line, every frame, every corner"; adjective-triplet stacks — "indexed, queryable, memeable") and **narrative explanation** (copy that tells the user the design's philosophy, or performs personality on behalf of the machine).

**Defaults:** labels not sentences ("placed 12:34" not a two-clause explanation of what placing means); counts not stories ("3 elsewhere"); one plain declarative sentence for taglines/descriptions, slightly dry, no hype. The rulings behind a design belong in code comments and docs, never on the surface. Show candidate copy before applying it in more than one place.
