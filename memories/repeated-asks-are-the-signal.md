**On the Nth ask of the same question, don't re-answer from today's snapshot.** The user's intuition is seeing a trend the per-day evaluation keeps missing. Before answering: find the prior asks and answers; tally what the "rejected" alternative would have saved since the first ask (incidents, modules, workarounds); if the tally is growing, the honest answer is a costed spike or an actual build — not a re-run of the old reasoning. Say plainly when a previous answer aged badly; the correction is worth more than the consistency.

**The disguise to watch for:** acknowledging the Nth ask while still sequencing the thing behind one more precursor step — deferral wearing a receipt. On the Nth ask, build or act now; any "manual step first" becomes verification OF the built thing, never its prerequisite.
