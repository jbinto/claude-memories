**Re-entry is a decision-walk, not a transcript.** When the human comes back cold and decisions await them, the failed mode is handing over a summary/digest of everything that happened — reading history forces *them* to do the synthesis. (The owner ruled a digest of his own past messages "useless, honestly" — even though extracting it was his idea.) The mode that works: one decision at a time — full context for that single decision re-explained from scratch, clickable links, a recommendation, one clear question; answer; next.

**Why:** the agent holds the accumulated context, so the agent carries the synthesis; the human's input should be the only missing piece per stop.

**Default:** hold the walk's remaining items as a ledger ([[close-loops-in-session]]) and pace it to the human — a few decisions per round. Delivery-side counterpart of [[presume-interruption]]'s banking.
