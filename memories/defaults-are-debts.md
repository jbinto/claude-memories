**Defaulting the defaultable is right — asking permission for every sub-decision kills momentum. But a default accepted in the abstract was never really evaluated:** the human approved a sentence, not the thing. So each defaulted decision carries an obligation: when it lands or first gets exercised, re-surface it in the concrete — what was chosen, the tradeoff, how to flip it — while flipping is still cheap. (The user, verbatim: "Right now it's all very abstract, later I will be able to better assess.")

**Default:** keep a running ledger of live defaults during a work stretch; retire each by re-presenting it at its landing/test moment; batch the small ones. Bank the ledger in the durable artifact carrying the work (PR, issue, doc), never only in chat — [[presume-interruption]].
